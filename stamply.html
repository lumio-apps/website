Stamply

Do I need a visa? Find out in seconds, even offline. Visa and entry rules for 199 passports to every destination, in 20 languages.

Android · 20 languages · Works offline · GitHub only

Download APK Source code

Screenshots may show an older look. The app now uses the Lite design, in light and dark.

Why Stamply

Fast

Two taps and you see the rule for any passport and destination.

Offline

All data is inside the app. It works at the airport with no signal.

Private

No ads, no tracking, no account. Your passport and trips never leave your phone.

More than one answer

Compare passports, plan a trip, track Schengen 90/180 days and get passport expiry reminders.

Features

Check

  • Pick your passport and destination from a searchable list of 199 countries with flags, and swap them with one tap
  • A result card styled like a passport stamp: visa-free, visa on arrival, ETA, e-Visa or visa required
  • Share the result as a picture
  • Allowed stay in days, where it is known
  • Before you fly tips, and a warning about fake e-Visa websites
  • Passport expiry check against the common 6-month rule
  • Exchange rate: tap Refresh to see 1 USD in the local currency (and your passport currency). The last rate is kept offline with its date
  • Official website link: opens a hand-checked government page, only after you confirm
  • Country facts: the capital, currency, calling code and languages of the destination, offline

Passports

  • Passport power: how many destinations you reach without a visa in advance, and your rank among 199 passports
  • World ranking of all passports
  • Second passport for dual citizens: switch with one tap, and the result tells you when your other passport is easier
  • Compare two passports side by side

Map, widget and shortcuts

  • Offline world map: every country colored by what your passport needs. Tap a country, pinch to zoom
  • Home-screen widget with your passport and how many destinations it reaches
  • App shortcuts: long-press the icon for Check, Explore, World map and Schengen

Trips

  • Trip planner: add several countries and see the rule for each, and how many need something before you fly
  • Schengen 90/180 calculator: add past and planned stays, see days used and left, and when a day frees up. Works fully offline
  • Add a country to your trip from any result, and share the whole trip as text

Explore and saved

  • Every destination for your passport in one list, with filters by status and search
  • Bookmark trips from the result screen. A Changed badge appears when a data update changes the rule
  • Regions: filter the list by Africa, Americas, Asia, Europe or Oceania
  • Surprise me picks a random place you can visit without applying for a visa first, within the region you chose

Look and settings

  • Lite design: flat, light screens with thin borders, no heavy shadows and a bottom bar with icons and labels, so it feels fast even on older phones. Works in light and dark
  • Passport expiry date with optional reminders 6, 3 and 1 month before, set on your device
  • Update data downloads the latest visa data with one tap, or turn on the optional automatic data check (off by default, at most once a day)
  • Backup and restore: copy or share your passports, saved checks, trip, Schengen stays and settings as plain text, then restore them on another phone. Nothing is uploaded
  • Check for updates looks on GitHub for a newer app version when you tap it

20 languages

English, हिन्दी, বাংলা, ગુજરાતી, ಕನ್ನಡ, മലയാളം, मराठी, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, தமிழ், తెలుగు, اردو, العربية, 中文, 日本語, Español, Français, Português, Русский, Bahasa Indonesia, Հայերեն. Country names are translated too, so you can search in your own language. Arabic and Urdu use a right-to-left layout.

How the data works

  • Built in: all visa data ships inside the app, so Stamply works with no internet at all.
  • Optional update: Settings › Update data downloads one static file from GitHub. No server, no account, no personal data sent. An optional automatic check, off by default, does the same when the app opens, at most once a day.
  • Refreshed monthly: the file is rebuilt on the 1st of every month from the open Passport Index Data.

General guidance only. Visa rules change often. Always confirm with the embassy or the official government website of your destination before you travel.

Private by design

No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no account. Your passport, saved trips and recent checks stay on your device. The internet is used only when you tap Update data, Check for updates or Refresh on an exchange rate, or if you turn on the optional automatic data check. Read the full privacy policy.

Download

From GitHub: get the signed APK from Releases (Stamply-X.Y.Z.apk) and allow "install from this source" when Android asks.

GitHub only, on purpose. The code, the data and the downloads all stay open and under our control. To update, tap Settings › Check for updates or install a newer APK over the old one.
