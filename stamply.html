Stamply

Do I need a visa? Find out in seconds, even offline. Visa and entry rules for 199 passports to every destination.

Android · Works offline · Free

Download APK Source code on GitHub

Screenshots

Features

Check

  • Pick your passport and destination from a searchable list of 199 countries, and swap them with one tap
  • A clear result card styled like a passport stamp: visa-free, visa on arrival, ETA, e-Visa or visa required
  • Allowed stay in days, where it is known
  • Before you fly tips: passport validity, return ticket, proof of funds, and a warning about fake e-Visa websites
  • Recent checks with their status at a glance

Explore

  • Every destination for your passport in one list
  • See how many countries you can visit without a visa in advance
  • Filter by status and search any destination

Saved

Bookmark trips from the result screen, and swipe to remove them.

Settings

  • Light, dark or system theme
  • Update data: download the latest visa data with one tap
  • Check for updates for the app itself

How the data works

  • Built in

    All visa data ships inside the app, so Stamply works with no internet at all, even at the airport.

  • Optional update

    Settings › Update data downloads one static file from GitHub. No server, no account and no personal data sent.

  • Refreshed monthly

    The data file is rebuilt on the 1st of every month from the open Passport Index Data.

General guidance only. Visa rules change often. Always confirm with the embassy or the official government website of your destination before you travel.

Private by design

No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no account. Your passport, saved trips and recent checks stay on your device. The internet is used only when you tap "Update data" or "Check for updates". Read the full privacy policy.

Install

  1. Open the latest release on GitHub.
  2. Download the APK and open it on your phone.
  3. If asked, allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.
