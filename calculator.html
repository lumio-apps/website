Calculator

A clean, fast and fully offline calculator with a glass design. Scientific mode, unit converter, everyday tools and a date calculator in one small app.

Android · Free · No ads

Download APK Source code

Screenshots

Features

Calculator

  • Live result preview while you type
  • Scientific mode: sin, cos, tan, ln, log, √, x², xʸ, 1/x, π and e, in degrees or radians
  • Opens when you rotate your phone
  • History saved on your device; tap an entry to reuse it
  • Long-press the display to copy, paste or share; swipe it to delete the last digit

Unit converter

11 categories with live results for every unit at once: length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, time, data, fuel economy, pressure and energy.

Everyday tools

  • Discount and tax: sale price, savings, VAT, GST or sales tax
  • Tip and split: the tip and each person's share
  • Percentage: X% of Y, X as a percent of Y, percent change
  • Loan and EMI: monthly payment, total interest and total paid
  • BMI: metric or imperial, with the healthy range

Date calculator

  • Days between two dates, in years, months, weeks and days
  • Exact age from a date of birth, the weekday you were born and days until your next birthday

Make it yours

  • Glass design with an aurora background, and a Reduce transparency switch for a solid look
  • Light, dark or system theme with 7 accent colors
  • Number formats for every region: 1,234.56 1.234,56 1 234,56 12,34,567.89
  • Choose the decimal places; key vibration on or off

Private by design

Calculator works completely offline. It has no ads, no analytics and no accounts, and it never collects or sends your data. It makes one request to GitHub only when you tap Check for updates. Read the full privacy policy.

Install

  1. Open the latest release on GitHub.
  2. Download Calculator-vX.Y.Z.apk and open it on your phone.
  3. If asked, allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.

Already installed? Open the menu and tap Check for updates.

GitHub only, on purpose. Calculator is shared only on GitHub, so the code, the downloads and the updates all stay open and under our control.
