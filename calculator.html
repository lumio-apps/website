Calculator

A clean, fast and fully offline calculator for Android. Scientific mode, unit converter, everyday tools and a date calculator in one small app.

  • No ads
  • No tracking

Features

Calculator

  • Live result preview while you type
  • Scientific mode: sin, cos, tan, ln, log, √, x², xʸ, 1/x, π and e, in degrees or radians
  • Opens automatically when you rotate your phone
  • History saved on your device; tap an entry to reuse it
  • Long-press the display to copy, paste or share
  • Swipe the display to delete the last digit

Unit converter

11 categories with live results for every unit at once: Length, Weight, Temperature, Area, Volume, Speed, Time, Data, Fuel economy, Pressure and Energy.

Everyday tools

  • Discount & Tax: sale price, savings, VAT / GST / sales tax
  • Tip & Split: tip amount and each person's share
  • Percentage: X% of Y, X as a percent of Y, percent change
  • Loan / EMI: monthly payment, total interest and total paid
  • BMI: metric or imperial, with the healthy range

Date calculator

  • Days between two dates, in years, months, weeks and days
  • Exact age from a date of birth, the weekday you were born and days until your next birthday

Make it yours

  • Light, dark or system theme with 6 accent colors
  • Number formats for every region: 1,234.56 1.234,56 1 234,56 12,34,567.89
  • Choose how many decimal places to show
  • Key vibration on or off

Private by design

Calculator works completely offline. It has no ads, no analytics and no accounts, and it never collects or sends your data. The only network request happens when you tap "Check for updates", which asks GitHub whether a newer version exists.

Download and install

  1. Open the latest release on GitHub.
  2. Download Calculator-vX.Y.Z.apk and open it on your phone.
  3. If asked, allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.

Already installed? Open Menu → Check for updates inside the app.
