Paperwise

Paperwise creates new files and never changes your originals, but you are responsible for checking the result. Locking a PDF with a password protects it only as well as the password you choose; do not forget it, because we cannot recover it. Removing photo details or blurring parts of a picture is a best effort, so check the result before you share it. Text recognition (OCR) can make mistakes, so check the result before you rely on it. Do not use any tool on files you have no right to use.

Peekless

Peekless shows what an app is allowed to do, based on its permissions and the trackers found in its manifest. It cannot see what an app really does, it will miss some trackers, and a score is a guide, not a verdict. The optional Network monitor sees DNS lookups only: it can miss apps that use encrypted DNS (DNS over HTTPS) and does not work with a fixed Private DNS provider. Do not remove or distrust an app only because of its score. Check first.

Muster

Muster helps you keep attendance and work out salaries, but you are responsible for the result. Check the amounts before you pay anyone, and follow the labour and employee-record rules that apply to you. It is not accounting, payroll or legal software. A lost backup password or recovery code cannot be recovered, and the PIN lock protects the window, not the database file. Keep your own backups.
