Cuboo

A calm block puzzle for Android. Drag pieces onto the board, clear rows and columns and keep your combo going. No ads, no tracking, no accounts, and it works fully offline.

Android · Free · No ads

Download APK Source code

Why Cuboo

Calm to play

Drag three pieces onto an 8×8 board and clear full rows and columns. Back-to-back clears build a combo and multiply your score.

Private by design

No ads, no analytics, no accounts, no trackers. Scores and stats stay on your phone. The only internet use is the Check for updates button.

Your way to play

Four modes, hints, bombs, undo, a left-handed mode, light and dark themes, five block colour sets and three block styles.

Free forever

Open source under the MIT license. No ads, no in-app purchases and no locked features.

Features

Game modes

  • Classic: the normal game, it ends when no piece fits
  • Relaxed: never ends. When you are stuck, the board is cleared. Tap a piece to turn it
  • Timed: score as much as you can in 2 minutes
  • Daily Challenge: the same pieces for everyone today. Play it as often as you like
  • Every mode keeps its own best score

Gameplay

  • Hints show the move that clears the most lines (three per game)
  • Bombs show up now and then and blow up the 3×3 area around them
  • Undo takes back your last move (once per game)
  • Left-handed mode mirrors the top bar and the pieces
  • Your running game is saved, so you can leave and continue later

Look and feel

  • Light, dark or system theme
  • 5 block colour sets: Classic, Candy, Ocean, Forest and Mono
  • 3 block styles: Flat, Glossy and Outline
  • Smooth animations, sparks and a little screen shake on big clears. They switch off when your phone asks to reduce motion
  • Short sound effects and vibration, each with its own switch

Progress

  • Stats: best score, games played, average score, lines cleared and best combo
  • Recent scores: your last 10 games
  • 9 achievements to unlock
  • Scores, stats and achievements stay after you close the app, and your best score is saved after every move
  • If the phone ever refuses to open its normal storage, Cuboo saves into a small file in its own private folder instead

How scoring works

  • Placing a piece: 1 point per block.
  • Clearing lines: lines × lines × 10 × combo, so clearing two lines at once is worth four times as much as one.
  • Bomb: 5 points per block it destroys.

Private by design

No ads, no analytics, no accounts and no trackers. Scores, stats, achievements, settings and your running game stay only on your phone. They survive closing and updating the app and are removed only if you uninstall Cuboo or clear its data. The only network request is when you tap Check for updates. Sounds are bundled in the app. Read the full privacy policy.

Download

  1. Open the latest release on GitHub.
  2. Download Cuboo-vX.Y.Z.apk and open it on your phone.
  3. If asked, allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.

GitHub only, on purpose. To update, open Settings › Check for updates. Updates keep your scores, stats and settings.
