Cuboo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026
Developer: Lumio Apps
Source code: github.com/lumio-apps/cuboo

In short: Cuboo works offline and collects nothing. Your scores, stats and achievements stay on your phone. The internet is used only when you tap Check for updates.

Data we collect

None. Cuboo does not collect, store on a server, transmit, share or sell any personal or usage data. Lumio Apps runs no servers for this app.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking
  • No third-party services that collect data

Data stored on your device

Your scores, stats, achievements, recent scores, running game and settings (theme, block colours and style, sound, vibration and left-handed mode) are stored only on your device. They survive closing and updating the app. Nothing is sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

This data never leaves your device through Cuboo. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Internet access

Cuboo works fully offline and is shared only on GitHub, on purpose. It has no automatic update check. Only when you tap Settings › Check for updates, the app asks the public GitHub API whether a newer version exists and, if you agree, downloads it from GitHub. No personal data is sent. GitHub sees the request like any web request (for example your IP address). GitHub's own privacy statement applies to these requests: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Sounds

The sound effects are bundled inside the app. Nothing is downloaded while you play.

Children

Cuboo is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your own device. To remove it, uninstall the app. Files you saved or shared yourself stay where you put them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at https://github.com/lumio-apps/cuboo/issues or see the contact page.

This policy applies to Cuboo only. For the other Lumio apps and this website, see the main Privacy Policy.
