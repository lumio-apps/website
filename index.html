The honest part

Offline first. We tell you exactly when the internet is used.

Every feature that matters works with no connection. A few apps reach out to GitHub or a public data file, only for the things listed here, and never send your text, files or personal data.

  • All appsAsk GitHub for a newer version when you tap Check for updates. Somno and Heliora can also check once a day, which you can switch off.
  • PeeklessTap to update its tracker list from GitHub. The optional Network monitor is a local VPN for DNS lookups only, and nothing leaves your phone. The list of your apps is never sent.
  • MusterOnly the Check for updates button talks to GitHub, and an update is installed only after its digital signature is verified. Worker data never leaves the PC.
  • VertoLoads its language list when it opens and downloads a pack when you tap Download. Your text is never sent.
  • StamplyLoads visa data and an exchange rate when you tap, plus an optional daily data check that is off by default.
  • AnatomoDownloads its optional 3D pack once, when you tap Download.
  • HelioraThe space station, Tiangong, Hubble, rocket launches and space news use public space-data services, only if you turn on Online features.

Are Lumio apps really offline?

Yes. Every feature works without internet. The internet is used only to ask GitHub for a newer version when you tap Check for updates, plus the few small things listed under “The honest part”. None of it sends your personal data.

Are the apps completely free?

Yes. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no premium version.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your own device: your phone, or your Windows computer for Muster. There is no Lumio server, and nothing is uploaded.
