Lockeye

A private vault for Android that catches whoever guesses wrong. Type a wrong PIN and the front camera quietly takes a photo. Offline, no account, no ads, no tracking.

Android · Works offline · GitHub only

Download APK Source code

Screenshots

Why Lockeye

Catches wrong guesses

A wrong PIN takes a front camera photo, whoever typed it. You see who tried, and when.

Two PINs, two spaces

Your PIN opens everything. An optional Family PIN opens only the Family space, never your vault or the intruder photos.

Private by design

No account, no cloud, no ads, no analytics. PINs are stored only as salted hashes and photos never leave your phone.

Open source

MIT license. Read every line, build it yourself, tell us what to fix.

Features

Photo on every wrong PIN

  • A wrong PIN in Lockeye takes a front camera photo, family included
  • The camera opens only for the photo and closes right after, so Android's green camera dot does not stay on the lock screen
  • After 5 wrong tries in a row the keypad waits 30 seconds, then longer, up to 5 minutes
  • You can turn the photo off in Settings

Phone lock screen (optional)

  • Lockeye can also photograph wrong attempts on your phone's own lock screen: a wrong PIN, pattern or password
  • A guided four-step setup: device admin, show over other apps, camera and notifications, and keeping Lockeye awake
  • Uses Android's device admin with only the "watch login attempts" policy. Lockeye cannot read or change your PIN, and cannot lock or wipe the phone
  • Only wrong PINs, patterns and passwords count. Android does not report wrong fingerprints or faces

Vault

  • Photos from your gallery, files of any type and notes with a title and text
  • Kept in the app's private folder, so other apps and the gallery cannot see them
  • Your originals stay where they were, so delete them from the gallery yourself if you want them gone

Intruder log

  • Every wrong attempt, newest first, with the time, the attempt number, where it happened (Lockeye or the phone lock screen) and the photo
  • New attempts are marked, and the Intruders tab shows a badge
  • Tap a photo to view it, or delete one or all

Made for everyday use

  • Auto lock the moment you leave the app, closing any open photo, note or dialog
  • Light, dark or system theme
  • In-app updates: check, download and install a new version without leaving the app. Your PINs, vault and settings stay as they are

Good to know

  • Some phones (mostly Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo and Realme) block camera use on the lock screen even with every step done. The attempt is still logged, but without a photo.
  • To uninstall Lockeye after setting up the phone lock screen, first open Settings › Phone lock screen › Turn off and remove device admin. Android does not allow removing an app that is a device admin.
  • Lockeye is a deterrent and a way to find out who tried. It is not a replacement for your phone's own lock screen.

Private by design

Lockeye collects nothing. No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. PINs are stored only as salted hashes, and the photos, files and notes stay in the app's private folder on your phone. The internet is used only when you tap Check for updates, and nothing about you is sent. The optional phone lock screen features ask for extra permissions only if you set them up. Read the full privacy policy.

Install

  1. Open the latest release on your phone.
  2. Download Lockeye-vX.Y.Z.apk and open it. Android may ask once to allow installs from your browser.
  3. Open Lockeye and choose your PIN.

GitHub only, on purpose. Later versions install from inside the app, in Settings › Updates.
