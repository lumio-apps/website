PocketTally

Every coin, counted. A simple daily income and expense tracker that works completely offline, so your money data never leaves your device.

Android, Windows and Linux · 20 languages · 27 currencies · Free

Download Source code on GitHub

Screenshots

Why PocketTally

  • Made for daily life

    Add an expense in a few seconds, split a bill with friends, keep track of money you lent and see where your money went this month.

  • For everyone

    20 languages, 10 of them Indian, plus 27 currencies and local number formats like 12,34,567.89.

  • Free forever

    Open source under GPL-3.0. No premium version and no locked features.

Features

Track

  • Income (daily, weekly or monthly) and expenses in one list
  • Quick add with the + button: expense, income, lend and borrow, or a savings goal
  • Simple math in the amount field, like 120+45, and duplicate any entry
  • Recurring entries like salary or rent, added for you
  • Favorites for one-tap entries, tags like #trip, and bill photos

Understand

  • Reports for any week, month or year: net savings, the change from the period before, a chart by category, and income against expenses over time
  • Highlights like "You spent 12% less than last month"
  • Calendar view and upcoming bills
  • Search and filter by text, type, category, date and amount
  • PDF report in your language for any period, even a whole year, made on your device

Plan

  • Budgets: a monthly limit and a limit per category, with warnings and optional carry-over of what's left
  • Lend and borrow: who owes you, whom you owe, part payments and reminders
  • Split bill: share an expense with friends, equally or with your own amounts
  • Savings goals with progress

Your data, your way

  • Backup and restore to a file you choose, with or without bill photos
  • Auto backup every day or week, keeping the last 5
  • CSV export and import for Excel, LibreOffice or Google Sheets
  • Categories: add, rename, recolor, reorder, hide or delete

Private and handy

  • App lock with a 4-digit PIN
  • Daily reminder on days you haven't added anything (Android)
  • A big and a small home screen widget with this month's balance and quick add buttons (Android)
  • Shortcuts: long-press the app icon for "Add expense" or "Add income" (Android)
  • On computers: a wide layout with a sidebar, Ctrl+N for a new entry and Ctrl+F to search

Make it yours

  • A profile with your photo and name
  • Light, dark or system theme and six accent colors
  • Number formats: 1,234.56 1.234,56 1 234,56 1'234.56 12,34,567.89
  • Week starts on Monday or Sunday

Private by design

Everything is stored on your device in a local database. No account, no ads, no analytics. The app lock PIN is kept only as a salted hash, and backups, CSV files and PDF reports are saved only where you choose. The internet is used only when you tap "Check for updates".

Download

Take the file for your system from the latest release on GitHub. After the first install, the app keeps itself up to date from Settings › Check for updates.

SystemFileNotes
Androidpockettally-vX.Y.Z-android.apkWorks on every phone. Allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager when asked. It installs over an older PocketTally and keeps your data.
Windowspockettally-vX.Y.Z-windows-setup.exeInstalls for your user, no admin rights needed. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
Linuxpockettally-vX.Y.Z-linux-x86_64.AppImageOne file, no install. Make it executable with chmod +x and run it.

To move your data between a phone and a computer, use Back up and Restore in Settings.

Updating from a version older than 1.3.1 on Android? Version 1.3.1 is signed with a new key, so Android can't install it over an older PocketTally. Open the old app, make a backup in Settings, uninstall it, install 1.3.1 or newer and restore the backup.
