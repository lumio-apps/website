PocketTally

Every coin, counted. A simple income and expense tracker that works completely offline. No account, no ads, no tracking, and the only internet use is an update check you start yourself.

Android 7.0 or newer · 20 languages · 27 currencies · Free

Download APK Source code

Screenshots

The look is frosted glass on a soft gradient in the color of the accent you pick (six to choose from), in light and dark mode.

Why PocketTally

Private by design

No sign-up, no cloud, no ads, no analytics. The only internet use is the Check for updates button, which sends no data about you.

Quick

Add an expense in a few seconds, split a bill with friends, keep track of money you lent and see where your money went.

For everyone

20 languages, 10 of them Indian, 27 currencies and local number formats like 12,34,567.89.

Free forever

Open source under GPL-3.0. No premium version and no locked features.

Features

Track

  • Income (daily, weekly or monthly) and expenses in one list
  • Quick add with +: expense, income, lend and borrow, or a savings goal
  • Simple math in the amount field, like 120+45, and duplicate any entry
  • Recurring entries like salary or rent
  • Favorites, tags like #trip and bill photos

Understand

  • Reports for any week, month or year: net savings, change from the period before, a donut by category and income against expenses
  • Highlights like "You spent 12% less than last month"
  • Calendar view and upcoming bills
  • Search and filter by text, type, category, date and amount
  • PDF report in your language, made on your phone

Plan

  • Budgets: a monthly limit and a limit per category, with warnings and optional carry-over
  • Lend and borrow: who owes you, whom you owe, part payments and reminders
  • Split bill: share an expense with friends, equally or with your own amounts
  • Savings goals with progress

Your data, your way

  • Backup and restore to a file you choose, with or without bill photos
  • Auto backup every day or week, the last 5 kept
  • CSV export and import for Excel, LibreOffice or Google Sheets
  • Categories: add, rename, recolor, reorder, hide or delete

Private and handy

  • App lock with a 4-digit PIN
  • Daily reminder on days you haven't added anything
  • Home screen widgets with this month's balance and quick add buttons
  • Shortcuts: long-press the app icon for Add expense or Add income
  • A wide layout with a sidebar on tablets and big screens

Make it yours

  • A profile with your photo and name
  • Light, dark or system theme and six accent colors
  • Number formats: 1,234.56 1.234,56 1 234,56 1'234.56 12,34,567.89
  • Week starts on Monday or Sunday

Private by design

Everything is stored on your device in a local database. No account, no ads, no analytics. The internet is used only when you tap Check for updates, and nothing about you is sent. The app lock PIN is kept only as a salted hash, and backups, CSV files and PDF reports are saved only where you choose. Read the full privacy policy.

Download

Get the APK from the latest release on GitHub: pockettally-vX.Y.Z-android.apk. It works on every phone with Android 7.0 or newer.

  1. Open the file on your phone and allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager when Android asks.
  2. To update, install the newer APK over the old one. Your data stays.

PocketTally has no automatic update check. Open Settings › Check for updates when you want to see if a newer version exists. PocketTally is shared only on GitHub, on purpose. Moving to a new phone? Use Settings › Back up data on the old one and Restore from backup on the new one.
