PocketTally

A simple daily income and expense tracker that keeps your money data on your own device.

Android, Windows and Linux · 13 languages · Free

Download Source code on GitHub

Screenshots

Features

Track every day

  • Add income (daily, weekly or monthly) and daily expenses
  • One list for both: income in green, expenses in red
  • Monthly balance, income and expenses on the home screen
  • Simple math in the amount field, like 120+45
  • Edit entries, or swipe to delete with Undo

See where money goes

  • Totals for any day, week, month or year
  • A chart by category and a month-by-month bar chart
  • Highlights like "You spent 12% less than last month"
  • Calendar view and upcoming bills
  • PDF report of any period, made on your device

Budgets and goals

  • A monthly limit and a limit per category, with warnings
  • Savings goals with progress
  • Lend and borrow: who owes you, whom you owe, part payments

Less typing

  • Recurring entries like salary or rent are added for you
  • Favorites add an entry with one tap
  • Tags like #trip, and search by text, type, category, date or amount
  • Your own categories: rename, recolor, reorder, hide or delete

Your data, your files

  • Back up and restore to a file you choose; nothing is uploaded
  • CSV export and import for Excel or Google Sheets
  • Bill photos attached to entries, kept only in the app

Made for every country

  • English, हिन्दी, Հայերեն, 中文, Español, العربية, Français, বাংলা, Português, Русский, اردو, Bahasa Indonesia, 日本語
  • Any currency, including INR, USD, EUR and AMD
  • Number formats: 1,234.56 1.234,56 1 234,56 12,34,567.89
  • Light or dark theme, five accent colors, week starts Monday or Sunday

Private by design

All your data stays on your device in a local database. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The internet is used only when you tap "Check for updates".

Download

Every version is on GitHub Releases. Pick the file for your device.

Android

File endingUse it for
arm64-v8a.apkAlmost all phones from the last 7 to 8 years
armeabi-v7a.apkOlder or low-cost phones
x86_64.apkEmulators and some tablets
universal.apkAny device, if you are not sure (bigger file)

After the first install, "Check for updates" in the app picks the right file for your phone.

Windows

Download windows-setup.exe to install it (no admin rights needed), or windows.zip to run it without installing. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.

Linux

Download the .AppImage, make it executable with chmod +x and open it. A .tar.gz is also available.

To move your data between a phone and a computer, use Back up and Restore in Settings.
