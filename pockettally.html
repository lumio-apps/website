PocketTally

A simple, offline daily income and expense tracker for Android, Windows and Linux. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Features

Track everything

  • Add income (daily, weekly or monthly) and daily expenses
  • Income and expenses in one list: income green, expense red
  • Monthly balance, income and expenses on the home screen
  • Simple math in the amount field, like 120+45
  • Edit entries; swipe to delete with Undo

Categories

Create your own categories, rename, recolor, reorder, hide or delete them.

Stats and reports

  • Totals for any day, week, month or year
  • Pie chart by category and month-by-month bar chart
  • Highlights like "You spent 12% less than last month"
  • PDF report of any period, made on your device
  • Calendar view and upcoming bills

Budgets

Set a monthly limit and a limit per category, with warnings before you overspend.

Save time

  • Recurring entries like salary or rent are added automatically
  • Favorites for one-tap entries
  • Tags like #trip, searchable
  • Search and filter by text, type, category, date range and amount

Money you owe or are owed

Lend & Borrow shows who owes you and whom you owe, including part payments. Savings goals show your progress.

Your data, your files

  • Backup and restore to a file you choose, nothing is uploaded
  • CSV export and import for Excel or Google Sheets
  • Bill photos attached to entries, stored only in the app

Make it yours

  • One app-wide currency (INR, USD, EUR, AMD and many more)
  • Number formats: 1,234.56 1.234,56 1 234,56 1'234.56 12,34,567.89
  • Light, dark or system theme and five accent colors
  • Week starts on Monday or Sunday

13 languages

English, हिन्दी, Հայերեն, 中文, Español, العربية, Français, বাংলা, Português, Русский, اردو, Bahasa Indonesia, 日本語

Private by design

All your data stays on your device in a local database. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The internet permission is used only when you tap "Check for updates", which contacts the GitHub Releases page of this project.

Download

Get the latest version from GitHub Releases.

Android

FileFor
arm64-v8a.apkAlmost all phones from the last 7 to 8 years
armeabi-v7a.apkOlder or low-cost phones (32-bit)
x86_64.apkEmulators and some tablets
universal.apkAny device (bigger file)

Not sure? Take the universal APK. After that, "Check for updates" in the app picks the smaller file for your phone automatically.

Windows

Take the windows-setup.exe (installs for your user, no admin rights needed) or the portable windows.zip (unzip and run pockettally.exe). Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app is not signed with a paid certificate: click More info → Run anyway.

Linux

Take the linux-x86_64.AppImage, make it executable with chmod +x pockettally-*.AppImage and run it. A .tar.gz is also available.

Tip: use Backup / Restore in Settings to move your data between a phone and a computer.
