Heliora

Explore the Solar System, offline first. Real planet positions, rotating 3D worlds, a star map for your sky, the Moon, eclipses and an honest answer to "could humans live here?"

Android · English and Hindi · Works offline · Free

Download APK Source code

Screenshots

Why Heliora

Real sky, real maths

Planet positions, the Moon, sunrise and sunset, eclipses and the star map are all worked out on your phone, not copied from a website.

Offline first

Everything works without internet. The few online extras are off until you switch them on.

Private by design

No ads, no account, no tracking. Your birth date, city and settings stay on your phone.

Open source

MIT license. No ads, no in-app purchases and no locked features.

Features

Explore

  • 2D map with real positions: pick any date from 1800 to 2050, step 30 days back or forward, or press play and watch the planets move
  • 3D worlds: rotate and zoom Earth with moving clouds, Jupiter's Great Red Spot, Saturn's rings, the glowing Sun and the cracked ice of Europa
  • 18 worlds: the Sun, all 8 planets, Pluto, our Moon and 7 famous moons

Tonight's sky

  • Which planets can I see tonight? Pick your city (no location permission) and see which planets are up after dark, the best time to look, the direction and how high, plus rise and set times
  • The Moon tonight and a Moon calendar with the dates and times of every new moon, first quarter, full moon and last quarter
  • The sky on any date, from 1900 to 2049
  • Sun and light: first light, sunrise, the golden hours, solar noon, sunset, last light, full dark and the length of the day
  • Star map: the sky above your city with 1,000 stars, 89 constellations, the Moon, the Sun and the planets. Drag to look around, pinch to zoom, tap anything for its name and where it is right now
  • Constellation stories for eleven famous constellations, and a binocular guide with twelve sights such as the Pleiades and the Andromeda Galaxy. All kept in the app, so the star map works offline
  • Space events for the next 12 months, and eclipses with a rough local guide. Tap the bell and Heliora reminds you the evening before, made on your phone. Always use eclipse glasses to look at the Sun
  • Share tonight's sky: one tap makes a picture with the Moon and the planets you can see from your city, made on the phone
  • Find it with your compass: hold your phone up and Heliora shows which way to turn and tilt to point at the Moon or a planet. Motion sensors only: no camera, no location
  • Tonight in the sky (optional): a short evening note about the Moon and the bright planets, worked out on your phone

Optional online features

  • Off by default. Turn on Online features in Settings to see where the International Space Station is right now on a map, when it passes over your city and whether you can see it
  • Tiangong and Hubble: where each one is now and when it flies over your city, with a bell for every pass
  • Rocket launches: the list of upcoming launches in the events calendar, with reminders
  • Space news: the latest headlines from the Spaceflight News API. Only text is downloaded, and a story opens on the publisher's own website
  • Orbit data is saved on the phone, so the station keeps working offline for days. Switch the setting off and Heliora never contacts these services

Learn

  • Planet facts: diameter, gravity, temperature, length of day and year, known moons, distance from the Sun and surface pressure
  • Atmosphere and oxygen: what each atmosphere is made of and how its oxygen compares with Earth's
  • Could humans live here? A simple 0 to 10 life score for every world. Earth scores 10, Mars 4, Jupiter 0
  • How far is it? Today's real distance from Earth, and how long light, a spacecraft or a car would need
  • Compare any two worlds side by side, with their true sizes drawn next to each other
  • Quiz in three levels, with your best score saved

You

  • Search and favourites: find planets, moons, missions and spacecraft, and star the ones you love
  • Your weight and your jump on every world, from the Moon to the Sun
  • Your age on other planets and the date of your next birthday there
  • My birthday sky: the Moon's phase and where the planets were on the day you were born, plus your 10,000th day and other round numbers
  • Spacecraft now: how far Voyager, New Horizons, Webb and the Mars rovers are from Earth, and how long a radio signal needs to reach them
  • Space missions: a timeline of 15 famous missions with a short story each
  • Share a planet's card as a picture and send it to your friends

Made for everyone

  • Light, dark or system theme, and a text size setting for easier reading
  • English and Hindi, with translated planet and constellation names. Earlier versions had 20 languages. From 1.16.0 the app is kept in the two that are fully proofread
  • In-app updates: new versions download inside the app with a progress bar, then the Android installer opens. Your settings are kept

Where the data comes from

  • Planet facts: NASA Planetary Fact Sheet.
  • Positions and distances: Keplerian orbital elements from JPL (Standish), valid 1800 to 2050 and solved on the phone.
  • Star map: about 1,000 stars and the constellation lines from d3-celestial (Hipparcos-based), kept inside the app.
  • Life score: six simple rules. It is a teaching aid, not science.
  • Space station, Tiangong and Hubble: orbit data downloaded and kept on the phone, positions worked out on the phone. Rocket launches: Launch Library 2. News: Spaceflight News API.

Private by design

No ads, no account and no tracking. Planet data, positions, the star map and 3D surfaces are all worked out on your phone. Your birth date, chosen city and settings stay on your device. Online features are off until you switch them on. Besides that, the internet is used only for updates: Heliora looks for a new version on GitHub once a day, which you can switch off in Settings, and downloads one only when you tap Download and install. Nothing about you is sent. Read the full privacy policy.

Install

  1. Open the latest release on your Android phone.
  2. Download Heliora-vX.Y.Z.apk and open it.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.

Updates: after that, Heliora updates itself. Open Settings › Check for updates, or let it check once a day.

F-Droid: Heliora is also prepared for F-Droid. F-Droid signs apps with its own key, so an F-Droid install and a GitHub install cannot update each other. Pick one source.
