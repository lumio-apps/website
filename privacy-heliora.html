Heliora Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026
Developer: Lumio Apps
Source code: github.com/lumio-apps/heliora

In short: Heliora works offline first and collects nothing. Your birth date, city and settings stay on your phone. Two optional online features are off until you switch them on.

Data we collect

None. Heliora does not collect, store on a server, transmit, share or sell any personal or usage data. Lumio Apps runs no servers for this app.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking
  • No third-party services that collect data

Data stored on your device

Your birth date (used only for your age on other planets), your chosen city (used only for tonight's sky), your event reminders, your best quiz score and your settings (theme, language, the daily update check and Online features) are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

This data never leaves your device through Heliora. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Internet access

Planet data, positions, tonight's sky and the 3D surfaces are all worked out on your phone. You pick your city from a list, so Heliora never asks for your location. The compass finder uses only the phone's motion sensors: no camera and no location.

Heliora is shared only on GitHub, on purpose. The internet is used in these cases:

  • Updates: once a day Heliora asks the public GitHub API whether a newer version exists. You can turn this off in Settings, or tap Check for updates yourself. The new APK is downloaded from GitHub only when you tap Download and install.
  • Online features (off by default): if you switch them on in Settings, Heliora contacts api.wheretheiss.at (or celestrak.org) for the space station's orbit data and ll.thespacedevs.com (Launch Library 2) for the rocket launch list. Nothing about you is sent. Those services can only see your phone's internet address, like any website. The orbit data is saved on the phone, so the station keeps working offline for days. Switch the setting off and Heliora never contacts them.

GitHub sees update requests like any web request (for example your IP address). GitHub's own privacy statement applies to these requests: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Share picture

The planet card you share is drawn on your phone and handed to the Android share sheet. You choose where it goes. Heliora never uploads it.

Children

Heliora is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your own device. To remove it, uninstall the app. Files you saved or shared yourself stay where you put them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at https://github.com/lumio-apps/heliora/issues or see the contact page.

This policy applies to Heliora only. For the other Lumio apps and this website, see the main Privacy Policy.
