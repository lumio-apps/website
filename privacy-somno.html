Somno Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026
Developer: Lumio Apps
Source code: github.com/lumio-apps/somno

In short: Somno keeps all sleep data on your phone. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. It looks for a new version on GitHub at most once a day, which you can switch off.

Data we collect

None. Somno does not collect, store on a server, transmit, share or sell any personal or usage data. Lumio Apps runs no servers for this app.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking
  • No third-party services that collect data

Data stored on your device

Your sleep sessions, the screen-off and unlock times used to find them, your birth year (to suggest a sleep goal) and your settings are stored in a local database and files on your device. Android auto-backup is switched off for the app, so this data is not copied to any cloud backup. Nothing about you or your sleep is ever sent. Uninstalling the app removes it.

This data never leaves your device through Somno. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Internet access

Somno is shared only on GitHub, on purpose. The only network use is the update check: Somno asks the public GitHub API whether a newer version exists. This happens at most once a day, and you can turn it off in Settings. You can also tap Check for updates yourself. If you agree, the new APK is downloaded from GitHub. No personal data and no sleep data are sent. GitHub sees the request like any web request (for example your IP address). GitHub's own privacy statement applies to these requests: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

How tracking works

Somno records only two system events with their time: screen off and user unlock. If you grant the optional Usage access, Somno reads the same two kinds of event from Android's own history, never which apps you use. It does not read your messages, apps, location, microphone or camera. Sleep sessions are worked out on your phone from those times.

The Phone tab

The Phone tab shows your battery and device facts (for example battery level, temperature, model and free storage). Android hands these to the app live while the tab is open. Somno does not save, share or upload them.

Not medical advice

Somno estimates sleep from phone use. It cannot tell light from deep sleep and does not diagnose anything. See the Terms and Disclaimer.

Children

Somno is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your own device. To remove it, uninstall the app. Files you saved or shared yourself stay where you put them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at https://github.com/lumio-apps/somno/issues or see the contact page.

This policy applies to Somno only. For the other Lumio apps and this website, see the main Privacy Policy.
