Privacy Policy

Short and honest: we do not collect your data.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short: Lumio Apps does not collect your data. Our apps run on your device, have no ads, no analytics and no accounts, and we run no servers that receive your information.

What all our apps have in common

  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking
  • No account or sign-in
  • Your data stays on your device
  • Shared only on GitHub, on purpose, so the code and the downloads stay open
  • The internet is used only to ask GitHub for newer files: Check for updates in every app and Update data in Stamply, when you tap them. A few small exceptions, none sending your personal data: Verto loads its language list when it opens and downloads language packs when you tap Download, Anatomo downloads its optional 3D pack when you tap Download, and Somno and Heliora look for a new version once a day (you can switch this off), Stamply can check for new visa data once a day if you switch that on and loads an exchange rate when you tap Refresh, and Heliora's space station and rocket launch features contact public space-data services only if you turn on Online features

This website

lumio-apps.online is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising on it. We load the Space Grotesk font from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends a request (including your IP address) to Google when a page opens. Our hosting provider, GitHub, may log visits under its own privacy statement. If you use the light/dark switch, your choice is saved only in your browser (local storage) so the site remembers it. It is never sent to us. Links to GitHub and other sites are governed by those sites' policies.
