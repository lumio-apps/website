Privacy Policy

Short and honest: we do not collect your data.

Last updated: 17 October 2026

In short: Lumio Apps does not collect your data. Our apps run on your device, have no ads, no analytics and no accounts, and we run no servers that receive your information.

Policy for each app

Each app has its own privacy policy, because the details (permissions, update checks) differ a little.

What all our apps have in common

  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking
  • No account or sign-in
  • Your data stays on your device
  • Shared only on GitHub, on purpose, so the code and the downloads stay open
  • The internet is used only when you tap a button: Check for updates in every app, and Update data in Stamply. Both only ask GitHub for a newer file

This website

lumio-apps.online is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising on it. We load the Outfit font from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends a request (including your IP address) to Google when a page opens. Our hosting provider, GitHub, may log visits under its own privacy statement. Links to GitHub and other sites are governed by those sites' policies.
