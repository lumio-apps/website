Pow2

Swipe. Merge. Keep growing. A calm, fast number-merging puzzle for Android with no ads, no tracking and no accounts. It works fully offline.

Android · Free · No ads

Download APK Source code

Why Pow2

Calm and endless

Slide the tiles, merge equal numbers and make bigger ones: 2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 4 = 8. There is no fixed goal, so you play for your own best.

Private by design

No ads, no analytics, no accounts, no trackers. Scores and stats stay on your phone. The only internet use is the Check for updates button.

Your way to play

Four modes, four board sizes, undo, a hint button and four themes. Animations turn off when your phone's Remove animations is on.

Free forever

Open source under the MIT license. No ads, no in-app purchases and no locked features.

Features

Modes

  • Classic: play until no moves are left
  • Timed: 3 minutes, the clock starts with your first move
  • Zen: no game over, a full board clears its smallest tiles
  • Daily: one 4×4 board per day, the same for everyone, with a streak

Board and help

  • Board sizes 3×3, 4×4, 5×5 and 6×6
  • Undo up to 3 moves (not in the daily challenge)
  • Hint: a light bulb button shows a good next swipe

Progress

  • 17 achievements, from Warm-up to Week streak
  • Stats: best score for every mode and size, biggest tile, games played, total moves and daily streak

Look and feel

  • Four themes: Midnight, Daylight, Ocean and Candy
  • Smooth slides, merge pops and a counting score, with optional sound and vibration
  • Share your score as a picture when a game ends

Your data

  • Backup: copy your progress as text and restore it on another phone
  • Scores, stats, achievements and your running game are stored only on your phone

How to play

  • Swipe anywhere on the screen to slide all tiles in that direction.
  • Two tiles with the same number merge into one with double the value.
  • After every move a new 2 (sometimes a 4) appears.
  • The game ends when the board is full and nothing can merge.

Tap the mode chip, for example Classic · 4×4, to change the mode or the board size.

Private by design

No ads, no analytics, no accounts and no trackers. Scores, stats, achievements and your running game stay only on your phone. The only network request is when you tap Check for updates. The share picture is drawn on your phone and handed to the Android share sheet, and you choose where it goes. Read the full privacy policy.

Download

  1. Open the latest release on GitHub.
  2. Download the .apk and open it on your phone.
  3. If asked, allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.

GitHub only, on purpose. To update, open Settings › Check for updates. Pow2 looks for a newer release on GitHub and installs it over the old one. Your scores, stats and achievements stay.
