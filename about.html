About Lumio Apps

Small apps that respect you.

No ads. No tracking. Not now, not ever. We will never put ads or trackers in any Lumio app.

What we do

Lumio Apps makes small, useful tools that run entirely on your device. Our idea is simple: an app should do its job and stay out of your way. No ads, no tracking, no accounts and nothing sent to a server.

Our apps

  • CalculatorScientific mode, unit converter and everyday money tools
  • PocketTallyIncome, expenses and budgets in 20 languages
  • PaperwiseA private PDF toolkit
  • StamplyVisa rules for 199 passports, offline
  • Pow2A calm number-merging puzzle game

What we believe

  • Offline first. Every feature works without internet.
  • Private by design. We cannot see your data, because it never leaves your phone.
  • Open source. All code is public on GitHub. Read it, build it, improve it.
  • Free. No premium version and no locked features.
  • For everyone. Many languages, including many Indian languages, and local number formats.

Where to get the apps

You can get our apps from GitHub Releases, and only there, on purpose. That way the code, the downloads and the updates all stay open and under our control, and no store sits between you and the apps.
