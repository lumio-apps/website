Stamply Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026
Developer: Lumio Apps
Source code: github.com/lumio-apps/stamply

In short: Stamply collects nothing. Your passport choice, saved trips and recent checks stay on your phone. The internet is used only when you tap Update data or Check for updates.

Data we collect

None. Stamply does not collect, store on a server, transmit, share or sell any personal or usage data. Lumio Apps runs no servers for this app.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking
  • No third-party services that collect data

Data stored on your device

Your chosen passport, your passport expiry date (if you add it), your recent and saved checks, your trips and Schengen stays, your language and your theme are saved only on your device. The home-screen widget and app shortcuts show your saved passport and recent checks, which also stays on your device. You can clear recent checks in the app.

This data never leaves your device through Stamply. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Internet access

Stamply works fully offline. Visa data is built into the app.

Stamply is shared only on GitHub, on purpose. The app connects to the internet only when you tap a button:

  • Update data downloads the latest public visa data file from GitHub. Nothing about you or your checks is sent.
  • Check for updates asks the public GitHub API whether a newer app version exists and, if you agree, downloads it from GitHub. No personal data is sent.

GitHub's own privacy statement applies to these requests: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Visa information is guidance only

Stamply shows general visa and entry information. It is not legal advice and it can be out of date. Always confirm with the official government website or embassy of your destination. See the Terms and Disclaimer.

Children

Stamply is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your own device. To remove it, uninstall the app. Files you saved or shared yourself stay where you put them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at https://github.com/lumio-apps/stamply/issues or see the contact page.

This policy applies to Stamply only. For the other Lumio apps and this website, see the main Privacy Policy.
