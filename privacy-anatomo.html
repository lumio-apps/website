Anatomo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026
Developer: Lumio Apps
Source code: github.com/lumio-apps/anatomo

In short: Anatomo collects nothing. The 2D atlas works fully offline. The internet is used only to download the optional 3D pack and to check for updates, when you tap those buttons.

Data we collect

None. Anatomo does not collect, store on a server, transmit, share or sell any personal or usage data. Lumio Apps runs no servers for this app.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking
  • No third-party services that collect data

Data stored on your device

Your language, the body you chose (male or female), the layers you turned on, your favorites and your best quiz score are saved in a small file inside the app . They never leave your device. Updates keep them.

This data never leaves your device through Anatomo. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Internet access

Anatomo is shared only on GitHub, on purpose.

  • 2D view: the pictures of the body are inside the app. Exploring works fully offline.
  • 3D pack: only when you tap Download, the app downloads the 3D models from the pack-1 release of lumio-apps/anatomo and keeps them in its own folder. After that the 3D view works offline. It is drawn by a tiny web server inside the app, reachable only from your own device (127.0.0.1). You can remove the pack in Settings.
  • Check for updates: only when you tap it, the app asks the public GitHub API for the latest release and, if you agree, downloads the new APK from GitHub.

GitHub sees these requests like any web request (for example your IP address). GitHub's own privacy statement applies to these requests: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Learning only

Anatomo is for learning only. It is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor about your health. The anatomy data is credited in the attribution file.

Children

Anatomo is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your own device. To remove it, uninstall the app. Files you saved or shared yourself stay where you put them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at https://github.com/lumio-apps/anatomo/issues or see the contact page.

This policy applies to Anatomo only. For the other Lumio apps and this website, see the main Privacy Policy.
