Lockeye Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026
Developer: Lumio Apps
Source code: github.com/lumio-apps/lockeye

In short: Lockeye collects nothing. PINs are stored only as salted hashes, and the photos taken after wrong PINs never leave your phone. The only network use is the update check you start yourself.

Data we collect

None. Lockeye does not collect, store on a server, transmit, share or sell any personal or usage data. Lumio Apps runs no servers for this app.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking
  • No third-party services that collect data

Data stored on your device

Your PINs (saved only as salted hashes, never the PIN itself), your settings, the intruder log with the photos taken after wrong PIN attempts, and the photos, files and notes you add to the vault. All of it stays in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps and the gallery cannot see it. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

This data never leaves your device through Lockeye. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Internet access

Lockeye is shared only on GitHub, on purpose. It works completely offline. The only network use is the optional Updates button: Lockeye asks the public GitHub API whether a newer version exists and, if you agree, downloads the new APK from GitHub. No personal data is sent. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to these requests: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Photos of wrong attempts

When someone types a wrong PIN, Lockeye takes a photo with the front camera and saves it, with the time, in the intruder log. You can turn this off in Settings, and you can delete one photo or all of them. If you switch on the optional phone lock screen feature, the same happens after a wrong PIN, pattern or password on your phone's own lock screen. Nothing is uploaded.

Limits

Lockeye is a deterrent and a witness, not a safe. The vault is not encrypted yet. It is protected by your PIN and by Android's private app storage. Someone with full access to an unlocked or rooted phone can reach app storage. See the Terms and Disclaimer.

Children

Lockeye is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your own device. To remove it, uninstall the app. Files you saved or shared yourself stay where you put them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at https://github.com/lumio-apps/lockeye/issues or see the contact page.

This policy applies to Lockeye only. For the other Lumio apps and this website, see the main Privacy Policy.
