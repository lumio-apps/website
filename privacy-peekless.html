Peekless Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026
Developer: Lumio Apps
Source code: github.com/lumio-apps/peekless

In short: Peekless collects nothing. It scores your apps on your phone, and the list of your apps never leaves it. The optional Network monitor is a local VPN for DNS lookups only, and nothing leaves your phone. The only network use is the buttons you tap to look for updates or a newer tracker list, and it contacts GitHub only.

Data we collect

None. Peekless does not collect, store on a server, transmit, share or sell any personal or usage data. Lumio Apps runs no servers for this app.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking
  • No third-party services that collect data

Data stored on your device

Your settings, your trusted apps list, the score history behind the score-over-time chart, the optional Network monitor counts (which domain names each app asked for and how often), the results of the optional Deep scan, your report exports and the tracker list (the one shipped with the app, plus a newer one if you update it). All of it stays in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes it. Reports you export or share yourself stay where you put them.

This data never leaves your device through Peekless. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Internet access

Peekless is shared on GitHub and is also prepared for F-Droid. It works completely offline: reading your installed apps, their permissions and their trackers, and working out the scores all happen on your phone. The internet is used only when you tap one of these:

  • Check for updates: Peekless asks the public GitHub API whether a newer version exists and, if you agree, downloads the new APK from GitHub.
  • Update the tracker list: Peekless downloads a newer tracker list from raw.githubusercontent.com/lumio-apps/peekless. A downloaded list is checked first and can only add trackers, never remove them.

No personal data and no list of your apps is sent. GitHub sees these requests like any web request (for example your IP address). GitHub's own privacy statement applies to these requests: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Network monitor (optional, off by default). When you switch it on, Android asks you to allow a VPN connection. Peekless then runs a local VPN that takes only DNS lookups (one made-up DNS address is the only route), so your other traffic does not pass through it and your internet speed does not change. A lookup is passed on unchanged to your phone's own DNS server, and Peekless keeps only a count of the domain names each app asked for, on your phone. It never sees pages, messages or any content, it has no server, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else. You can clear the counts at any time and switch it off in Peekless or in Android settings.

What a score does and does not mean

Peekless shows what an app is allowed to do, not what it actually does. It finds trackers by reading each app's manifest, so some will be missed. A score is a guide, not a verdict. See the Terms and Disclaimer.

What the Network monitor cannot see

It sees DNS lookups only. It can miss apps that use their own encrypted DNS (DNS over HTTPS), it does not work when Android's Private DNS is set to a fixed provider, and showing which app asked needs Android 10 or newer. A domain that matches a tracker list shows that the app contacted that service, not what it sent.

Reports you export

A report (PDF or JSON) is created on your phone and handed to the Android share sheet. It lists your installed apps, so think before you send it. You choose where it goes. Peekless never uploads it.

Children

Peekless is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your own device. To remove it, uninstall the app. Files you saved or shared yourself stay where you put them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at https://github.com/lumio-apps/peekless/issues or see the contact page.

This policy applies to Peekless only. For the other Lumio apps and this website, see the main Privacy Policy.
