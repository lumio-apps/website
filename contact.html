When you write, please tell us

  • The app name and version (Settings or About screen)
  • Your phone model and Android version (for Muster: your Windows version)
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened
  • Do not include passwords or private documents

Help translate

PocketTally and Stamply are translated into 20 languages. Found a wrong word, or want to add a language? Edit the language file in the app's repository and open a pull request.
