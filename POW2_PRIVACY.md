# Privacy Policy for Pow2

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Pow2 is a number-merging puzzle game made by Lumio Apps. It works offline; the
only time it goes online is when you tap "Check for updates" (see Permissions).
**We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.** We run no
server, and the app has no account, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

## What stays on your phone

Your scores, stats, achievements, daily streak, your running game and your
settings (theme, sound and vibration) are stored only on your device. None of
this is sent anywhere.

## Share picture and backup

The share picture of your score is drawn on your phone and handed to the
Android share sheet. You choose where it goes. The backup is plain text that you
copy yourself. Pow2 never uploads either of them. If you send them to another
service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

## Permissions

- **Internet and install apps:** used only for **Settings > Check for
  updates**, and only when you tap it. The app then asks GitHub
  (api.github.com) for the newest Pow2 release, and if you choose to update it
  downloads the APK from GitHub and opens the Android installer. GitHub
  receives the usual technical data of any web request (such as your IP
  address), as with any website you open. The app sends none of your data and
  checks nothing in the background.

## Children

Pow2 is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your phone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
