Calculator Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026
Developer: Lumio Apps
Source code: github.com/lumio-apps/calculator

In short: Calculator works offline and collects nothing. Your history and settings stay on your phone. The internet is used only when you tap Check for updates.

Data we collect

None. Calculator does not collect, store on a server, transmit, share or sell any personal or usage data. Lumio Apps runs no servers for this app.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking
  • No third-party services that collect data

Data stored on your device

Your calculation history and your settings (theme, accent color, number format, decimal places and vibration) are saved only on your device. You can delete the history at any time in Settings › Clear history.

This data never leaves your device through Calculator. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Internet access

Calculator works completely offline. It is shared only on GitHub, on purpose, and has an optional Check for updates button. Only when you tap it, the app asks the public GitHub API whether a newer version exists and, if you agree, downloads it from GitHub. No personal data is sent. GitHub sees the request like any web request (for example your IP address). GitHub's own privacy statement applies to these requests: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Children

Calculator is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your own device. To remove it, uninstall the app. Files you saved or shared yourself stay where you put them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at https://github.com/lumio-apps/calculator/issues or see the contact page.

This policy applies to Calculator only. For the other Lumio apps and this website, see the main Privacy Policy.
