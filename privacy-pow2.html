Pow2 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026
Developer: Lumio Apps
Source code: github.com/lumio-apps/pow2

In short: Pow2 works offline and collects nothing. Your scores, stats and achievements stay on your phone. The internet is used only when you tap Check for updates.

Data we collect

None. Pow2 does not collect, store on a server, transmit, share or sell any personal or usage data. Lumio Apps runs no servers for this app.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking
  • No third-party services that collect data

Data stored on your device

Your scores, stats, achievements, daily streak, running game and settings (theme, sound and vibration) are saved in one small file in the app's private folder on your device. Other apps cannot read it. Nothing is sent anywhere. If you update from an older version, its saved data is moved into this file. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

This data never leaves your device through Pow2. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Internet access

Pow2 works fully offline and is shared only on GitHub, on purpose. It has no automatic update check. Only when you tap Settings › Check for updates, the app asks the public GitHub API whether a newer version exists and, if you agree, downloads it from GitHub. No personal data is sent. GitHub sees the request like any web request (for example your IP address). GitHub's own privacy statement applies to these requests: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Share picture

The share picture of your score is drawn on your phone and handed to the Android share sheet. You choose where it goes. Pow2 never uploads it. Pow2 has no backup feature, so there is no backup text or file.

Children

Pow2 is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your own device. To remove it, uninstall the app. Files you saved or shared yourself stay where you put them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at https://github.com/lumio-apps/pow2/issues or see the contact page.

This policy applies to Pow2 only. For the other Lumio apps and this website, see the main Privacy Policy.
