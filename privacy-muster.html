Muster Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026
Developer: Lumio Apps
Source code: github.com/lumio-apps/muster

In short: Muster collects nothing. Your workers' names, phone numbers, photos, attendance and pay stay on your computer. The only network use is the Check for updates button you press yourself.

Data we collect

None. Muster does not collect, store on a server, transmit, share or sell any personal or usage data. Lumio Apps runs no servers for this app.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking
  • No third-party services that collect data

Data stored on your device

Your workers' details (name, role, phone number, joining date and an optional photo), attendance, pay settings, advances, bonus and fines, salary records and your settings. They are kept in %APPDATA%\Muster on your computer: the database (muster.db), the worker photos and the safety copies made when you restore a backup. This is personal data about your workers. It is yours, and it is never sent to us.

This data never leaves your device through Muster. Muster has no uninstaller, so deleting its program folder does not delete this data.

Internet access

Muster is shared only on GitHub, on purpose. It works completely offline. The only network use is the optional Check for updates button: Muster asks GitHub (api.github.com) whether a newer version exists and, if you agree, downloads it from GitHub over HTTPS. Muster installs the update only after checking its digital signature (Ed25519). No personal data is sent. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to these requests: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Backups

A backup is a password-protected file (.mbk, AES-256-GCM) that you save yourself, wherever you choose. Muster does not upload it. If you lose the backup password or the recovery code, nobody can recover it, including us. When you restore a backup, Muster first moves your current data into a safety folder.

PIN lock and limits

The PIN lock protects the Muster window, and locks it when the computer is idle. It does not encrypt the database file on your disk, so anyone with access to your Windows account could read the file. Keep the computer itself safe. Encrypting the database is planned.

Your workers' data

You decide what you record about your workers. Keep it to what you need for attendance and pay, and follow the rules that apply to you for employee records. Reports you create (PDF or Excel) contain names and pay, so share them with care.

Children

Muster is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your own device. Muster keeps its data in %APPDATA%\Muster, so to remove it, close Muster and delete that folder yourself. Files you saved or shared yourself stay where you put them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at https://github.com/lumio-apps/muster/issues or see the contact page.

This policy applies to Muster only. For the other Lumio apps and this website, see the main Privacy Policy.
