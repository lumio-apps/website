Anatomo

Explore the human body in 2D and 3D. A small, free atlas that works offline, with no ads, no accounts and no tracking. For Android, in 13 languages.

Android · 13 languages · GitHub only

Download APK Source code

Screenshots

Screenshots may show an older look. Version 3 has a new design with four tabs.

Why Anatomo

Small

The app holds 2D pictures only. The 3D models are an optional one-time download of about 68 MB.

Private

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. No internet is needed after the download.

Open

Code under MIT, anatomy data under CC BY 4.0.

Everywhere

Any Android phone from Android 5.0, with 13 languages.

Features

Explore

  • Real bodies: a male body (about 2,300 parts) and a female body (about 1,500 parts) from open scientific anatomy data
  • Four tabs: Explore (the body), Parts (search and browse), Learn and Settings
  • 2D, light and fast: sharp pictures from the front, back and side. Pinch to zoom, tap any part
  • 3D pack: download it once from Settings to turn the body in 3D, pull it apart with Spread apart, look inside with Cross-section, let it turn by itself with Auto-rotate and show Labels
  • Layers: turn skin, muscles, bones, organs, nerves and vessels on or off

Learn

  • A page for every part: a picture, where it is in the body, what it does, what is good for it, what harms it, quick facts, the pieces it is made of and the parts it works with
  • Learn tab: an organ of the day, a mixed quiz with pictures, "find the part" on the body, a body systems quiz, flashcards and your progress
  • Favorites: tap the star on a part to keep it
  • Text size: make the text bigger or smaller in Settings
  • 13 languages: English, Hindi, Armenian, Russian, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Chinese, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Italian

Comfort

  • Dark and light mode that follows the phone, or pick one in Settings
  • Fast on phones: layers load only when you switch them on, and the Graphics setting (Auto, Low, Medium, High) fits the 3D view to your phone
  • Updates inside the app: Settings › Updates › Check. Your settings, favorites and progress are kept

Learning only

Not medical advice. Anatomo is for learning only. Talk to a doctor about your health.

The anatomy data comes from BodyParts3D 4.0 (© The Database Center for Life Science) and the Human Reference Atlas / HuBMAP female reference organ set v1.5, both CC BY 4.0. The female skeleton and muscles are adapted from the male body, so they show where parts sit, not exact female anatomy. Full details are in the attribution file.

Private by design

No ads, no accounts and no tracking. Your language, chosen body, layers, favorites, studied parts, text size and best quiz score are saved in a small file on your device. The internet is used only to download the optional 3D pack when you tap Download, and for Check for updates when you tap it. Read the full privacy policy.

Install

  1. Open the latest release on your phone.
  2. Download Anatomo-vX.Y.Z.apk and open it. Allow installing from this source when Android asks.

Android 5.0 or newer. For the 3D pack, a phone with 3 GB of RAM or more works best.

GitHub only, on purpose. To update, open Settings › Updates.
