PocketTally Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026
Developer: Lumio Apps
Source code: github.com/lumio-apps/pockettally

In short: PocketTally works offline and collects nothing. Your entries, budgets, goals and photos stay on your device. The internet is used only when you tap Check for updates.

Data we collect

None. PocketTally does not collect, store on a server, transmit, share or sell any personal or usage data. Lumio Apps runs no servers for this app.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking
  • No third-party services that collect data

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter is stored only on your device in a local database: entries, categories, budgets, lend and borrow records, savings goals, your name and settings, and the bill photos and profile photo you choose to add. The PIN of the app lock is stored only as a salted hash.

This data never leaves your device through PocketTally. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Internet access

PocketTally works offline and is shared only on GitHub, on purpose. It has no automatic update check. Only when you tap Settings › Check for updates, the app asks the public GitHub API whether a newer version exists and, if you agree, downloads it from GitHub. No personal data is sent. GitHub sees the request like any web request (for example your IP address). {GH}

Backups and exports

Backup files, CSV files and PDF reports are created only when you ask for them, or by the automatic backup you can switch on in Settings. They are saved on your device, in the place you choose (or in the Documents/PocketTally folder for automatic backups). The app never uploads them. If you move a file to another service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

Children

PocketTally is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your own device. To remove it, uninstall the app. Files you saved or shared yourself stay where you put them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at https://github.com/lumio-apps/pockettally/issues or see the contact page.

This policy applies to PocketTally only. For the other Lumio apps and this website, see the main Privacy Policy.
