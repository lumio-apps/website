Paperwise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026
Developer: Lumio Apps
Source code: github.com/lumio-apps/paperwise

In short: All PDF and photo work happens on your device. Your files are never uploaded. The internet is used only when you tap Check for updates.

Data we collect

None. Paperwise does not collect, store on a server, transmit, share or sell any personal or usage data. Lumio Apps runs no servers for this app.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking
  • No third-party services that collect data

Data stored on your device

The PDFs and pictures Paperwise creates, a small list of where you stopped reading and your favorites, your saved signature if you keep one, and your settings (look, language, file order). Passwords you type to lock a PDF are used only to protect that file and are never stored. Photos, PDFs and camera pictures are processed on the device.

This data never leaves your device through Paperwise. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Internet access

Your files are never uploaded. Paperwise is shared only on GitHub, on purpose, and has no automatic update check. Only when you tap Check for updates, the app asks the public GitHub API whether a newer version exists and, if you agree, downloads it from GitHub. No personal data and no files are sent. GitHub sees the request like any web request (for example your IP address). GitHub's own privacy statement applies to these requests: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Text recognition (OCR)

Text from scans uses a text recognition model bundled inside the app (Google ML Kit, on-device). The pictures of your pages are processed on your phone only and are never uploaded.

Backup

The backup feature creates one file on your phone. You choose where it goes. Paperwise does not upload it anywhere itself.

Files you pick

Paperwise opens files only when you choose them with the system file or photo picker, or when you open a PDF from another app. It works on a copy and never changes your original.

Children

Paperwise is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your own device. To remove it, uninstall the app. Files you saved or shared yourself stay where you put them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at https://github.com/lumio-apps/paperwise/issues or see the contact page.

This policy applies to Paperwise only. For the other Lumio apps and this website, see the main Privacy Policy.
