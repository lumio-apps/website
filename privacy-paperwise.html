Paperwise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026
Developer: Lumio Apps
Source code: github.com/lumio-apps/paperwise

In short: All PDF and photo work happens on your device. Your files are never uploaded. The internet is used only if you tap Check for updates in the GitHub version.

Data we collect

None. Paperwise does not collect, store on a server, transmit, share or sell any personal or usage data. Lumio Apps runs no servers for this app.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking
  • No third-party services that collect data

Data stored on your device

The PDFs and pictures you create are kept in the app's own folder until you share or save them. Paperwise also remembers your favorites and the page where you stopped reading. Passwords you type to lock or unlock a PDF are used only for that action and are never stored. Photos, PDFs and camera pictures are processed on the device.

This data never leaves your device through Paperwise. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Internet access

Paperwise needs no internet to work. The GitHub version has a Check for updates button. Only when you tap it, the app asks the public GitHub API whether a newer version exists and, if you agree, downloads it. No personal data and none of your files are sent. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to these requests: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

The Google Play version (when published) will not contain the update check or the internet permission.

Files you pick

Paperwise opens files only when you choose them with the system file or photo picker, or when you open a PDF from another app. It works on a copy and never changes your original. Photo privacy tools remove location and device details from the copy they make.

Children

Paperwise is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your own device. To remove it, uninstall the app. Files you saved or shared yourself stay where you put them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at https://github.com/lumio-apps/paperwise/issues or see the contact page.

This policy applies to Paperwise only. For the other Lumio apps and this website, see the main Privacy Policy.
