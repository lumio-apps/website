# Privacy Policy

**App:** Calculator (package `com.lumioapps.calculator`)
**Developer:** Lumio Apps
**Last updated:** October 11, 2026

Calculator is built to respect your privacy.

## Data we collect

**None.** Calculator does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal or usage data.

- No accounts or sign-in
- No ads
- No analytics, crash reporting or tracking
- No third-party services that collect data

## Data stored on your device

Your calculation history and your settings (theme, accent color, number format, decimal places and vibration) are saved only on your device. They never leave it. You can delete the history at any time in **Settings → Clear history**, and uninstalling the app removes all of this data.

## Internet access

Calculator works completely offline and is shared only on GitHub, on purpose.

The app contains an optional **Check for updates** button. Only when you tap it, the app asks the public GitHub API whether a newer version exists and, if you agree, downloads it from GitHub. No personal data is sent. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to that request: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## Permissions

| Permission | Version | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Internet | Only for Check for updates | Check for updates when you tap the button |
| Install packages | Only for Check for updates | Install a downloaded update after you confirm |

## Children

Calculator does not collect data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
